# Critique findings decided against

Decisions the author took after a critique. A later critique should not raise them again unless the reason given no longer holds.

## Hiding the user's tools on the achievement card until hover

- **Finding (2026-10-08):** each pending card carries the checklist, note and pin controls even when they hold nothing; hiding them until hover or focus would make the card at rest shorter and the list denser.
- **Decision:** not done. The controls stay visible on every card.
- **Reason:** hiding controls behind hover hurts discovery, and the app is used with a mouse arriving from another monitor, so there is no resting hover to rely on. The gain would be about 10px a card, because the row of guide buttons stays.

## One guide button instead of three

- **Finding (2026-10-08):** three guide buttons (Steam guides, YouTube, Google) force a choice on every lookup; one button opening the last-used site, with the others behind a chevron, would be lighter.
- **Decision:** the three buttons stay. They became ghost buttons in muted text, which removed their visual weight.
- **Reason:** the author asked for the three sites as direct, one-click shortcuts.

## More visual character

- **Finding (2026-10-08):** the look is stock dark with Steam's colours and nothing expresses achievement hunting; rarity could have a visual treatment.
- **Decision:** the sobriety stays. The completion state is the one authored moment.
- **Reason:** the app sits beside a game and must not compete with it; restraint is the brief (see PRODUCT.md, "The game comes first").
