# Account card

One account, with everything about it in one place. A list of them shows
whose accounts the app has and switches between them.

## Anatomy

A card (Panel Navy, hairline, 8px): a 40px avatar, the name in title weight
with the SteamID under it in caption text, and the status of the key at the
end. A list ends with a dashed card of the same width, a plus and "Add account".

<Canvas of={AccountCardStories.InAList} />

## States

The account in use has a Signal Blue border at 60 percent and the words "In
use" beside its name. It is not a button, and it is where the card opens:
under a hairline, on the field colour, the masked key with its actions.

<Canvas of={AccountCardStories.InUse} />

Any other account is one button exactly as wide as the card. Its hover is a
wash and a lighter border, never the accent: a hover must not make an account
look like the one in use.

## Do and don't

- **Do** keep what belongs to an account inside its card.
- **Do** keep the card the same shape whether it is in use or not. What makes
  it clickable is a button laid under its content; when the card itself
  changed from a button to a plain element, every switch redrew both cards
  and the avatars flickered.
- **Don't** list an account's facts as loose rows under a row of faces:
  nothing ties them to the account, and a face-sized tile has more hover than
  content.
- **Don't** show the key. It appears only masked (see `MaskedKey`).
