# Notice

A message that stays on the page until its cause is gone, with the one action
that deals with it.

## Anatomy

A tinted, bordered box (6px): a title in medium weight, an optional line under
it in Quiet Steel, and one small button at its end.

## Variants

**Information** is a 10 percent wash and a 40 percent border of Signal Blue.
It is announced politely.

<Canvas of={NoticeStories.Information} />

**Problem** is the same in Erase Red, with a warning triangle at its start. It
is announced at once.

<Canvas of={NoticeStories.Problem} />
<Canvas of={NoticeStories.WithoutAction} />

## Do and don't

- **Do** keep its words in the text colour. The icon carries the meaning.
- **Don't** set the words in red: red text fails contrast on a red tint.
- **Don't** use it for something that goes away by itself in a moment; that is a toast.
- The unlock notice at the top of an achievement list is a different thing (a
  one-line status that is dismissed) and is not built on this.
