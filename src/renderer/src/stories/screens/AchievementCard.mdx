# Achievement card

The unit the whole app is built around.

## Anatomy

A 48px icon at the left; to its right the name in title weight, with the
rarity percentage in caption text at the far end; the description below at 75
percent opacity; then, when there is one, a progress bar with its count; and a
last row of ghost buttons, with guide searches at the left in Quiet Steel and
the user's own tools (checklist, note, pin) at the right.

<Canvas of={AchievementCardStories.Pending} />

A title and its description share one size: weight and opacity do the work,
which keeps a card short enough for a list to stay in view.

says which stat feeds it. The app never invents a value.

## What the user adds

Steam does not say which items are missing in a "collect them all". The
checklist, the note and the pin are where the user keeps that. They open
inside the card, growing to their height, and are saved half a second after
the last edit.

## Do and don't

- **Do** keep the card memoised and its handlers stable: a list has up to a
  hundred of these, and typing in one must not redraw the others.
- **Don't** hide the tools until hover: the app is read at a glance, beside a game.
- **Don't** colour a card to add variety. Amber is "set apart" (pinned,
  hidden) and green is "done".
